# [Thank You For Your Service](/games/thank-you-for-your-service/)

A scrolling receipt that tells the story of three years at a restaurant you built from nothing, and the renovation that happened while you were gone.

Thermal printer. Fake choices. One punchline.

**Format:** Web  
**Time:** 15-30 minutes

# [The Review](/games/the-review/)

What if a life had a Steam store page? Patch notes from v0.0, locked achievements, reviews from different perspectives, and a sleep schedule DLC that's been "coming soon" for decades.

Retro Steam aesthetic. Easter eggs for those who click around.

**Format:** Web  
**Time:** 15-30 minutes
